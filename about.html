About Kurious Movement

A community-first movement space in Albury–Wodonga—parkour, functional training, and play for every body.

More than classes

We’re building a place where people connect through movement—kids, teens, adults, and families. Expect community events, playful skill sessions, and clear progressions so everyone can start where they are and grow with confidence.

  • Community events & open sessions
  • Beginner-friendly progressions
  • Supportive, safety-first coaching

Our purpose

Our mission is to help people discover freedom in movement and confidence in community. We believe movement should be fun, skillful, and shared. We teach with curiosity, respect, and a bias toward safe, sustainable progress.

Guiding principles: safety first, scalable challenges, positive coaching, and inclusion. We want Kurious Movement to be a home base for active, curious people—from first steps to advanced flow.
